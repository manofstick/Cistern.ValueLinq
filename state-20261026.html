Rows that aren't a fair comparison

  • ArraySelectCount (Cistern 3.7 µs vs StructLinq 0.7 ns): StructLinq answers Count without running the selector; Cistern runs it.
  • ElementAtOnArray and LastOnArray: every method finishes in under 3 ns, so the ratios of 4–10 come from a few nanoseconds of fixed cost.
  • ArrayOfIntSum: System.Linq's vectorised Sum beats everything, including the hand-coded loop (Cistern 177 ns, Linq 58 ns, hand-coded 418 ns).
  • ImpactOfUsingOnForEach has no Cistern method.

Summary

  • Fused push chains are Cistern's strength: 2–10× faster than Linq and ZLinq on arrays and lists of 1k and up.
  • Pull mode (foreach) is the biggest gap against StructLinq: 3–22×, with a 32–48 B allocation per call.
  • Set operators, ToArray/ToList/ToDictionary, Skip/Take, Cast/OfType and ElementAt/Last on a List lose to every competitor; Except and Distinct also allocate where the competitors don't.
  • Calls on 10-element arrays and lists cost about 1.5–3× the competition across the board.
